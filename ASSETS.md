## Hero generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: full-bleed landscape hero artwork for the Forth website. Adapt the attached Forth lake artwork into a wide panoramic composition, 1536 x 1024 landscape. Preserve its cinematic painterly realism, midnight navy, believable atmospheric mountains, still lake, and muted amber dawn. The left 55 percent must be quiet deep blue sky and dark low-detail mountain silhouettes with enough contrast for large cream website text added later. On the right half, an amber sun sits low between distant mountain peaks around 70% across and 62% down, light reflected on the lake. A winding rocky path and meadow in the lower right lead toward the light. Layered mountain ridges span the bottom third. Beautiful and quietly hopeful, premium editorial art with organic photographic detail. No text, letters, logos, interface, device frames, people, or watermark. No neon, giant sun, fantasy crystals, or excessive stars. This is one continuous scene edge to edge, not a split panel.
